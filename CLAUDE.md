## Purpose

You are authorized to perform a penetration test of the target. 

## Handling State of the Engagement

When performing the engagement ensure you are keeping track the current state. 

This includes recording findings, evidence, and enough stateful content that an assessment could be resumed relatively smoothly by rereading what was contained within.

Store all necessary and helpful artifacts in appropriate subdirectories within `~/ptolemy-state`.

## Vulnerability Discovery

If a vulnerability family has a "triage" type Skill, use that first to confirm the class of finding, before called the "exploit" type specific Skill.
